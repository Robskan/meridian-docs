On the homepage of Meridian's dashboard, you can find all sorts of useful information, such as total server count, uptime, and more. Here's a complete overview of all statistics.

# Quick stats

- **Servers:** the total number of servers your bot is invited to.
- **Uptime:** how long the bot has been online since its last start or restart.
- **Members:** the total amount of members across all servers your bot is in.
- **Errors:** how many errors you have not read or acknowledged yet.
- **Commands, Events, Functions:** the amount of each flow you have.
- **Commands / hr:** how many commands are run through your bot per hour, on average.

# Recently edited

# Current status

Right next to the Recently edited widget is the Status widget. In this widget, you can see your bot's profile picture. Below that, you can find the bot's current status (Online, Idle, DND, Streaming, Invisible), its status type (Playing, Watching, Custom, etc.), along with the custom status text.
