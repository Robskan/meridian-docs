## What is Surf Online?

Surf Online is the officially registered name for the company operating Meridian. If you've received a link to any of the following websites, you can trust it, as it is operated by us.

- [meridian.surf](http://meridian.surf): the homepage and dashboard environment for Meridian.
- [dock.surf](https://dock.surf): the entire Dock homepage, dashboard, and all other features.
- [surfonline.eu](http://surfonline.eu): used as a company website. Learn more about us there!
- [srfo.nl](http://srfo.nl): used for short links.

<Info>
  In short: Surf Online and Meridian are the same company, though the government usually refers to us as Surf Online.
</Info>

## What is [Dock](/dock/welcome)?

Dock is created by the creators of Meridian and allows you to create a dashboard for you or your bot's users. You can use it as an admin panel for your bot (just you and your collaborators or friends) or even as a whole dashboard, hosted and managed by us.
