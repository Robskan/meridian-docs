## What is Surf Online?

Surf Online is the officially registered name for the company operating Meridian. If you've received a link to any of the following websites, you can trust it, as it is operated by us.

- [meridian.surf](http://meridian.surf): the homepage and dashboard environment for Meridian.
- [citrum.cc](http://citrum.cc): used as the homepage and dashboard editor for Citrum.
- [citrum.app](http://citrum.app): used as a subdomain for bot dashboards (e.g. `cool-bot.citrum.app`). 
- [surfonline.eu](http://surfonline.eu): used as a company website. Learn more about us there!
- [srfo.nl](http://srfo.nl): used for short links.

<Info>
  In short: Surf Online and Meridian are the same company, though the government usually refers to us as Surf Online.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Citrum, [citrum.cc](http://citrum.cc) and [citrum.app](http://citrum.app) are retired names. We are soon switching to -. The domains mentioned above will no longer be in use after June 4th, 2027. Until then, they will redirect to the new domains.
</Warning>

## What is Citrum?

Citrum is created by the creators of Meridian and allows you to create a dashboard for you or your bot's users. You can use it as an admin panel for your bot (just you and your collaborators or friends) or even as a whole dashboard, hosted and managed by us.
