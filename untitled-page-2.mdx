## The Form Editor

<Frame>
  ![Form Editor Modal](/images/Form-Editor-Modal.png "Form Editor Modal")
</Frame>

The form editor is where the majority of the configurations for the forms you make happen. It features a components panel on the left (much like the components panel in the [message editor](/builder/messages/send-a-message)), editing space in the middle, and configurations for the form or active component on the right.

### Available Components

Forms made on Meridian are always sent to Discord as Forms v2, v1 is not supported. The components available in Forms v2 include:
