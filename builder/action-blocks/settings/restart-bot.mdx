The **Restart Bot** block lives in the **Settings** palette category. Restart the bot process to clear memory or resolve hanging.

## What it does

Restart the bot process to clear memory or resolve hanging.



## Configuration

Open the block in the builder sidebar to configure these fields.

### `note`

**Type:** `note`.

Limited to two restarts per 30 minutes. Excessive restarts may trigger Discord rate limits.
