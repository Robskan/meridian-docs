The **Wait for interaction** block is in the **Other** category in the Meridian builder.

Pause until someone reacts, sends a message, or uses a component..

## Settings

### Listen for

Reaction, message, button press, dropdown selection, or modal submit.

### Who can trigger

Anyone, command user, a specific user, or members with a role.

### Timeout

Up to 30s on Free/Hobby. Up to 60s on Pro and Max.
