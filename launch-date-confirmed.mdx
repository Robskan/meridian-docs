Running a Discord server means a never-ending list of small things you wish your bot could do. A new command for your members. A welcome flow that actually fits your community. A small tweak to something that already works.

Usually, every one of those means finding a developer, waiting for them to have time, and hoping the result matches what you had in mind. We're building Meridian so you don't have to wait.

## Your server. Your bot. Your rules.

Meridian lets you build your own Discord bot without writing a single line of code. You decide what it does, how it looks, and how it behaves in your server.

Want to add a command? Build it. Want to change how something works? Change it. Your server is growing and your needs are shifting? Your bot can grow with it.

There's no code to maintain and no developer to chase, so the person who knows the community best is the one building the bot: you.

## Opening to everyone on January 15

Meridian has been in the hands of testers for a while now. They've tested it, broken it (way too many times), pushed it in directions we didn't expect, and told us what worked and what didn't.

On **January 15, 2027**, we're opening Meridian to the public.

## What happens between now and launch

The next few weeks are all about getting the details right. We're:

- Testing every part of Meridian, again and again and again...
- Fixing the issues our early users have reported
- Refining the experience based on their feedback

Our goal is simple. When you open Meridian on launch day, it should feel ready.

## Thank you to our early users

None of this would be possible without the people who joined us early. Every bug report, suggestion, and honest bit of feedback has shaped Meridian into what it is today.

To everyone who's been part of this from the start: **thank you**. You're a big part of what we're about to launch.
